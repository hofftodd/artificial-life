Strategies

Organisms per strategy. Each panel has its own scale, so rare strategies stay readable.

Generations

How many generations separate living organisms from the founders. Evolution needs many of these.

Arms race

Prey defences (mean among absorbers) against hunter weapons (mean among predators). Coevolution shows as the pairs rising together.

Environment

Each emitter's output over time. Seasons show as dips; compare them with the strategy counts above. Emitters in phase draw on top of each other; hover to see every value.

Spectrum vs. emitters

Share of the population per absorption-spectrum band over time (darker = more organisms). Lines trace each emitter's spectrum; adaptation shows as the population's bands following them.

Speciation

Each organism carries a neutral marker gene; kin and mates are chosen by similar markers. Separate bands in the marker heatmap are reproductively isolated groups — species.

Families

Descendants of each founder (Muller-style). The seven families with the most presence over the run are shown; the rest are grouped as Other.

Traits

Population mean (line) with the 10th–90th percentile band.
